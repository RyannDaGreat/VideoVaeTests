Ext. Japanese summer festival, night. A wide shot frames a large crowd of people gathered along a riverbank, gazing upward at a spectacular fireworks display blooming across the dark sky. Many figures wear traditional yukata and casual summer clothing, their silhouettes illuminated by bursts of gold, pink, blue, and white light from above. Low-rise buildings and streetlights line the left edge of the frame, and a calm river reflects the colorful explosions.

The camera holds a stationary wide angle from behind and slightly above the crowd, slowly pulling back as the fireworks intensify and then gradually fade. Successive bursts cascade in overlapping patterns — chrysanthemum shells, willow trails, and bright starbursts — casting flickering multicolored light across the upturned faces and shoulders of the spectators. The crowd shifts gently, some raising phones to capture the display.

As the show progresses, the camera drifts wider to reveal more of the riverbank and the distant treeline beyond the water. The final moments dim to a single rising trail and scattered embers against the night sky, leaving the crowd in near-darkness with only the glow of distant streetlamps and building windows. Ambient festival chatter, muffled booms, and the crackling hiss of pyrotechnics fill the soundscape throughout.
